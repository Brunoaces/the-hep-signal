# The HEP Signal

**Finding the signal in the background.**

The HEP Signal is an independent, weekly guide to noteworthy new research in high-energy physics phenomenology. Every Friday, we highlight papers announced that week on [arXiv hep-ph](https://arxiv.org/list/hep-ph/new), explain their scientific contributions, and identify questions worth following.

**Good science should not get lost in the background.**

## Read The HEP Signal

- **[Website and free newsletter](https://thehepsignal.netlify.app/)** — subscribe to the Friday edition.
- **[Scientific review archive](https://thehepsignal.netlify.app/archive/)** — browse our published paper assessments by subject, author, and arXiv identifier.
- **[Past editions](https://thehepsignal.netlify.app/issues/)** — revisit our weekly selections and editorials.

The archive begins with our first published issue; empty categories are intentional until reviews have been approved.

## What we publish

Each issue features **The Strongest Signal**, 6–9 additional **Signals Worth Following**, 3–5 **Honorable Mentions**, and a closing perspective called **Beyond the Background**.

We survey the full scope of hep-ph rather than applying quotas to individual research areas. Our editorial assessment considers originality, technical contribution, significance, limitations, and potential consequences for theory or experiment. A selection is an invitation to read, not a judgment on unselected work.

The reviews are independent commentary on preprints. They are **not peer review**, do not certify the correctness of every calculation, and are not affiliated with or endorsed by arXiv.

## Editorial independence and submissions

**The HEP Signal is an editor-curated publication, not an open-submission service.** We do not operate a public queue for manuscript recommendations, solicit coverage requests, or offer reviews on demand.

Scientific selections, corrections, and publication decisions are managed internally. This public repository provides transparency about the website's source code; **it is not an invitation to open GitHub Issues, Discussions, or external pull requests**. Repository maintenance is limited to authorized collaborators.

## About the repository

The website is statically generated from approved review records and published with Netlify. Subscriber registration and email delivery are handled by EmailOctopus; subscriber data is not stored in this repository.

Technical setup, content formats, validation rules, and release procedures are documented in **[docs/MAINTAINERS.md](docs/MAINTAINERS.md)**.
