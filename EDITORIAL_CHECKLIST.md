# The HEP Signal — Editorial gate before publishing a paper review

1. Is this manuscript **first announced in the covered week**, rather than an older update disguised as a new paper? Cross-lists should be clearly labeled when relevant.
2. Does the exact **arXiv ID / title / author list / date** match the actual manuscript?
3. Was the scientific claim checked beyond the abstract when feasible? Distinguish an **author's result** from an independent verification.
4. What is genuinely new compared with the relevant literature? What assumptions or approximations are essential?
5. Are there quantitative results, theoretical implications, or experimental consequences worth highlighting?
6. Is the tone calibrated, without promising breakthroughs, excluding entire subfields, or treating nonselected papers as low quality?
7. Assign one or more of the eight **topic IDs**. No forced quota per field.
8. Set `paper.status = "approved"` only after scientific editorial signoff and `issue.status = "published"` only when publicly releasing the issue. Drafts are excluded from the website.
9. Check the GitHub/Netlify Deploy Preview before merging. Confirm the site homepage, archive filters, new review permalinks, and actual subscribe form.
10. Publish the accompanying HTML newsletter separately in EmailOctopus, after a test email. The site review archive does not send emails by itself.
