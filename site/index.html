Why we exist

Not another list of abstracts.

New arXiv papers arrive every week, faster than any one researcher can read. The HEP Signal is a curated reading guide: a way to discover substantive work outside your immediate specialty and understand why it may matter. A selection is an invitation to read—not a judgment on everything left out.

01 / SURVEY

One week. The whole category.

We examine new hep-ph submissions across the field, rather than rotating among favored topics or research groups.

02 / EXTRACT

Scientific substance first.

Originality, methodological advances, relevance and testability matter more than name recognition or attention-grabbing claims.

03 / INTERPRET

Beyond the abstract.

We explain the contribution, why it matters, important caveats and what the community should watch next.

Our editorial standard

Discover the signal.
Keep the nuance.

Our signal-versus-background language is a metaphor for navigating a large body of research, not a numerical significance test or a statement that other manuscripts lack value.

We aim to reward originality, meaningful technical progress, reproducibility and credible paths to new knowledge. Our selections are informed editorial assessments, not peer review or a guarantee that every claim has been independently verified.
